Oura GPT

Oura GPT is a personal project that helps an authorized user review and understand their own Oura data.

How it works

  • You connect your Oura account through Oura's OAuth authorization flow.
  • The project accesses only the Oura data covered by the permissions you approve, which may include sleep, readiness, activity, and related health and wellness metrics.
  • AI analysis is optional and runs only when you ask for it. Data selected for an AI request may be sent to the AI service used to generate the response.

This project is not a medical device and does not provide medical diagnosis or treatment. It is not affiliated with or endorsed by Oura Health Oy.
