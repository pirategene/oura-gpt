Privacy Policy

Effective date: November 12, 2026

This policy describes how the personal Oura GPT project processes data when an authorized user connects an Oura account. The public informational pages themselves do not request access to Oura data.

Data processed

After you approve Oura's OAuth authorization, the project may receive an OAuth access token and the Oura data permitted by the scopes you approve. Depending on those permissions, this may include sleep, readiness, activity, heart-rate, workout, session, tag, and related health and wellness data, together with basic Oura account information.

How data is used

Data is used to retrieve, display, summarize, and help you interpret your own Oura information, and to maintain the authorized connection. The project is intended for personal wellness use, not medical diagnosis or treatment.

Optional AI analysis

AI analysis occurs only when you initiate a request. The Oura data selected for that request, along with your prompt, may be sent to the AI service used to generate the response. That service processes the submitted information under its own applicable terms and privacy practices. Do not request AI analysis if you do not want the selected data sent to that service.

Storage and retention

The project may process or retain OAuth tokens, selected Oura data, prompts, or generated results as needed for the particular deployment and requested features. Exact retention depends on the implementation and hosting configuration. This policy does not make a guarantee about encryption, anonymization, or a specific retention period.

Sharing

Information may be handled by service providers required to operate the project, including Oura, hosting infrastructure, and—only for user-initiated analysis—the relevant AI service. The project is not intended to sell personal Oura data or use it for advertising.

Your choices

You can decline permissions, stop using the project, or revoke its access from your Oura account settings. You may request deletion of data controlled by the project through the repository's contact channel. Do not post health data, access tokens, or other sensitive information in a public issue.

Changes and contact

This policy may be updated as the project changes. Questions can be raised through the project repository.
